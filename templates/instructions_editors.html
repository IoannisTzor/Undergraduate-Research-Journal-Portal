Instructions for editors

Your job is to manage each work as it goes through the process, and to be the link between administrators, peer reviewers and submitters.

As an editor, you will:

  • check the formatting of submissions
  • assign submissions to peer reviewers
  • check reviewers' recommendations
  • send decisions to submitters
  • send finished works to the advisory board
  • add finished works to the journal
  • answer reviewers' questions while they work on their assignments

Checking formatting

The journal accepts four submission types, listed in the submission guidelines. Every submission must follow APA 7th Edition formatting, and each type has its own requirements for length and file type.

If a submission doesn't meet the formatting guidelines, send the submitter a rejection explaining the issues with formatting. Otherwise, your next step is to assign it to a peer reviewer.

On the site

  1. All submissions lists every submission. Bold "None assigned" marks work waiting for a reviewer.
  2. On a submission's page, check its files, then assign reviewers. Reviewers whose preferred discipline matches appear first.
  3. When reviews come in, read them, and approve each rubric once you've checked it doesn't reveal the reviewer's name.
  4. Send your decision with a message to the author. After corrections, you can assign reviewers again for the next round.
  5. For the final check, email an advisor from the Advisors list shown on the submission's page.
  6. On Build an issue, choose accepted works, upload the issue PDF, and publish.

Reviewers follow the instructions for reviewers.
