About the journal

[Journal Name] aims to showcase the incredible STEM research done by undergraduates at CMU. Founded in 2026 as a capstone project, the journal accepts submissions from any CMU undergraduate student who wishes to share their work. We do our best to highlight their accomplishments while still providing an introduction to the peer review and publishing process, so those interested in graduate work are more prepared for their future roles.

All submissions approved by our editors go through a semi-formal peer review process. All of our staff, including editors and peer reviewers, are students, so the journal team has the opportunity to experience the publishing side of academia. Before publishing, peer reviewed and approved papers are sent to our advisory team of professors, to confirm the legitimacy of the research.
